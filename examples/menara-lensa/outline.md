# Outline: Menara Lensa

Framework: Story Circle (You, Need, Go, Search, Find, Take, Return, Change). Pattern: branch and bottleneck.

### arrival Iona at the door
- chars: Mira, Iona
- goal: Iona wants the spare lens.
- conflict: Mira does not trust strangers with the tower.
- outcome: No, and Iona reveals she knows about the dam.
- shift: safe to threatened
- setup: dam

### roof The roof instrument
- chars: Mira, Iona
- goal: Mira wants proof the charts are real.
- conflict: The sky is clouded; the instrument is broken.
- outcome: Yes, but the lens is the only way to fix it.
- shift: doubt to fear

### choice The lens
- chars: Mira, Iona
- goal: Mira must decide.
- conflict: Giving the lens leaves the tower without a spare.
- outcome: The player decides; both paths end the night.
- shift: alone to trusting, or safe to isolated
- payoff: dam
